## Before publishing
* **Verify every verse and reference** against the licensed edition you quote (wording of translations varies); the passages
  above are standard, widely cited ones, but the editorial sign-off is a human step.
* The Google Play button is a text stand-in. Replace it with Google's official badge from the Play brand guidelines.
* Artwork: the Gita / Ramayana / Mahabharata posters reuse the project's illustrated Hindu pack; Bible, Quran, Dhammapada and
  the multi-tradition poster use the project's original generated art (symbols and architecture only, no depiction of persons).
  Ramayana uses a temple scene rather than a depiction of Rama.
* `source/screen_home.png` is a real app screenshot (debug build, test ad slot hidden).
