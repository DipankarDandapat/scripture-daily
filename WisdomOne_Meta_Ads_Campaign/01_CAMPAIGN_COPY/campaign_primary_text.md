# WisdomOne — Campaign Primary Text Variations

Campaign-level primary texts for testing and creative refresh. The 25 ads launch with the per-ad primary text in
`tools/marketing/campaign_spec.py`; use these variations as alternatives (for example as additional text options in
Ads Manager, or when refreshing a fatigued ad). They are not a replacement for the spec copy.

## Rules applied

- **Front-load the message.** Meta usually truncates primary text after roughly 125 characters in Feed (with a *See more*
  link), and less in some placements. The product ("WisdomOne", "Bible verse") and the core benefit appear before
  that point in every variation.
- **Describe the app, never the reader.** No text asserts or implies the reader's religion, beliefs or state of mind
  (Meta personal-attributes policy). Test: the sentence must stay true for a reader with no religious belief.
- **Only real features:** daily Bible verse, lock-screen verse wallpaper, live wallpaper, refresh every 1 / 6 / 12 / 24 h,
  20 artworks and colour themes, reminders, Explain like I'm 5, Wisdom Tree (a missed day pauses it), light / dark design.
- **No claims** of results, rankings or prices other than "free". No implied paid tier.
- Character counts include spaces and punctuation; typographic apostrophes and dashes count as one character.
