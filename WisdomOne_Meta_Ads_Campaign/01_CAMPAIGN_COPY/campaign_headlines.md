## Notes

- Headlines 1–5 are the examples from the campaign brief; "Discover WisdomOne" is already the launch headline of
  `AS05_AD05_better_experience`.
- "Wisdom, Always With You" describes the app being present on the lock and home screens — it must not be changed to
  "Your faith, always with you", which presumes the reader's belief.
- Pair lock-screen headlines (6–8 above, "Inspiration on Every Unlock") only with creatives that show a lock screen.
- "Grow Your Wisdom Tree" needs a creative that shows the tree (e.g. `AS04_AD05_small_moments`) — otherwise the
  name means nothing to a new viewer.
- "Free Daily Bible Verse App" is accurate (the app is free); do not add "best", "#1" or similar ranking words.
